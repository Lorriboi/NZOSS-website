Who we are

Making the optometry journey even better.

NZOSS is a student-led society for past and present University of Auckland optometry students. We help students connect across year groups, learn from the profession and make the most of their university experience.

🎉

Build connections

Meet fellow students beyond your year group through socials, celebrations and shared experiences.
